How to Play

You and your partner (across the table) play against the two players on your left and right. First team to the target score wins.

  • BidEveryone bids how many books (tricks) they'll take. Your bid and your partner's add up to your team's bid. Bid Nil to take none at all: +100 if you do it, −100 if you don't.
  • PlayFollow the suit that was led if you can. Spades are always trump. You can't lead spades until one has been played.
  • ScoreMake your bid: 10 points per book bid, +1 for each extra book (a bag). Miss it: lose 10 per book bid. Every 10 bags costs 100 points.
  • TapTap a card in your hand to play it. Cards you can't play are dimmed.
