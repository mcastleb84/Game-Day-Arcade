How to Play

Gain 10 yards in 4 downs for a first down, or the game is over. A touchdown scores 7 points and moves you up a level.

  • HikeSnap the ball within 25 seconds or lose 5 yards.
  • ThrowLEFT or RIGHT THROW passes to that receiver. Or tap the field to aim.
  • JoystickDodge the rusher. After a catch, steer the runner.
  • Watch outHold the ball too long and you're sacked. An interception ends the game.
  • LevelsLevel 4: Linemen button adds a blocker and a rusher. Levels 6, 8 and 12 bring in new players.
