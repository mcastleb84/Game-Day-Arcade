Last Bowl

How to Play

Twelve teams, one trophy. You against 11 computer teams. Win a game and you move on. Lose and you're out. Win 4 in a row to take the Last Bowl.

  • WinFirst team to 21 points wins. A touchdown is 7. Teams trade drives.
  • DrivesEvery drive starts at the 50. Gain 10 yards in 4 downs or the other team gets the ball.
  • OffenseHIKE, then LEFT or RIGHT THROW, or tap the field to aim. Joystick moves the QB, then your runner.
  • DefenseThe blue-ringed player is yours. Joystick takes over; let go and he plays on his own. SWITCH or tap a player to change. Run into the ball carrier to tackle.
  • Round 13 on 3: QB and 2 receivers vs a pass rusher and 2 defensive backs. No blockers, so the rusher waits for a count.
  • LaterEach round adds players: center and safety, then running back and linebacker, then a guard and a 2nd rusher.
