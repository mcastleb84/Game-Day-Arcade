How to Play

Sink all 10 of the CPU's blue cups before it sinks your red ones. Win to move up a level. Lose a game and your run is over.

  • ThrowSwipe up on the table and let go. A longer swipe throws farther. Swipe a little left or right to aim.
  • TurnsYou get 2 balls a turn, then the CPU shoots 2 at your cups.
  • Balls backSink both balls in one turn and you get 2 more.
  • Re-rackWhen 6 or 3 cups are left, they get pushed back into a tight triangle.
  • LevelsEach level the CPU gets more accurate.
