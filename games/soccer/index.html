How to Play

Score 3 goals out of 5 attempts to beat the level. Miss too many and your run is over.

  • MovePut your finger anywhere on the field and drag. A joystick appears under your thumb.
  • ShootTap SHOOT. The ball goes the way your player is facing, so steer toward a corner of the goal.
  • LevelsThe goalie gets quicker every level. A defender joins at level 3, another at level 6, and a third at level 8.
  • PassFrom level 8 you get a teammate. Tap PASS to send the ball to them and take control of them.
  • ClockYou have 12 seconds to get a shot off on each attempt.
