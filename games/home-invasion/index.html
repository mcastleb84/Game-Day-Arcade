Home Invasion

Two strangers just kicked in the doors of your house. Fight them off, then survive every level after. Each level is harder than the last.

  • ThemTwo body shots turn a person into a zombie. Two more shots and the zombie goes down. A head shot kills right away.
  • YouGet hit once and you stumble and turn into a zombie. Get hit again and you fall and die. Clearing a level cures you.
  • GunsYou start with a knife, a handgun and a shotgun. Walk over any weapon lying around, or one an enemy drops, to grab it. Out of ammo? Fight with your fists.
  • PhoneLeft thumb moves. Drag on the right side to aim (up for head shots). Hold FIRE to shoot. Tap a weapon at the bottom to switch.
  • KeysW A S D move, mouse aims, click shoots, 1–8 or Q / E switch weapons.
