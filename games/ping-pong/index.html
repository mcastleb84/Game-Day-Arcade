How to Play

First to 7 points wins the game. Beat the CPU to move up a level. Lose a game and your run is over.

  • MoveDrag your finger left and right on the table to move your red paddle at the bottom.
  • ServeServes switch every 2 points. On your serve, tap the table or the SERVE button.
  • AimHit the ball with the edge of your paddle for a sharper angle. The middle sends it straight.
  • LevelsEach level the CPU gets quicker, the ball gets faster and your paddle gets a little smaller.
