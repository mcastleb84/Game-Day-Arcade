How to Play

Find every sports word hidden in the grid. Each puzzle you finish moves you up a level.

  • FindDrag your finger from the first letter of a word to the last, then let go.
  • DirectionsWords run across and down at first. Later levels add diagonals and backwards words.
  • LevelsThe grid gets bigger and there are more words to find as you go.
  • SavedYour level is saved, so you can pick up where you left off.
