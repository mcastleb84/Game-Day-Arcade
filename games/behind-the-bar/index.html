How to Play

You're the bartender. Customers walk up and order mixed drinks. Remember who ordered what, then make every drink from memory.

  • OrdersEach order shows the drink and what goes in it. Study them before the timer runs out, or tap I'M READY.
  • MixThe orders disappear. For each customer, tap the ingredients that go in their drink, then SERVE.
  • RoundsRound 1 is one customer. Every round adds one more person at the bar.
  • The boss1st wrong drink: a warning. 2nd: one more chance. 3rd: you're fired.
